Privacy Policy for Clip4M

Last updated: [DATE]

Clip4M is a personal content automation tool designed to assist the developer in uploading and managing video content on TikTok accounts owned by the developer.

Information We Collect

Clip4M does not collect, store, or share personal data from users. The application only processes video files, captions, and metadata provided directly by the developer for the purpose of uploading content to TikTok.

Use of Information

Any information processed by Clip4M is used solely to facilitate content uploads and is not shared with third parties.

Third-Party Services

Clip4M interacts with TikTok services strictly for content publishing purposes and operates in compliance with TikTok’s platform policies.

Data Retention

Clip4M does not retain personal data.
